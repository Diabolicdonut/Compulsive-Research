## Editorial method

The wave does not erase strong earlier reporting merely to claim a blank-page rewrite. Instead, it performs a substantive editorial revision: every non-benchmark page receives a card-specific thesis, practical play guidance, counterplay, and a tournament/cultural summary. Existing sections remain when they are specific and useful.

## Deck provenance

Recorded event lists remain `tournament-registration`. Representative, style-based, novelty, and community reconstructions are now `curated-example` with review and date metadata. Official commercial products remain separately identified. Lord of the Pit and Shahrazad explicitly disclose that their historical value is not supported by a conventional sanctioned tournament lineage.
