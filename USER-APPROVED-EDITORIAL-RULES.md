# User-Approved Editorial Rules

## Scope and cadence

- Rewrite/review numbered entries in archive waves of 50.
- Numbered Wave 01 is entries 001–050 and is approved.
- Continue with Numbered Wave 02, entries 051–100.
- Honorable Mentions are a separate 505-article track in 50-article waves, preserving landing-page category order and category-local navigation.

## Content

- Every article must be about its specific card, not interchangeable template prose.
- Include design/original-set context, historical importance, format impact, play guidance, counterplay, art/printing history, and lore where supported.
- Add a meaningful Tournament Scene or competitive-history section where evidence exists.
- Do not shorten existing articles simply to insert the new material.
- Preserve strong bespoke prose when it remains accurate and useful.
- Length follows evidence; do not pad articles to meet a universal reading-time target.

## Deck archives

- Include genuine historical tournament decks where available.
- Never invent a player, event, finish, date, or decklist.
- A documentary deck may appear in multiple card articles when genuinely relevant.
- Clearly identify reconstructions and representative shells as `curated-example`.
- Do not publish `editorial-study` decks.
- All visible card names in decklists must retain card-image previews; double-faced cards must show both faces.
- Preserve official Wizards commercial preconstructed-product sections even when no sideboard exists.

## Site behavior

- Eternal Dragon controls the canonical numbered-article visual structure.
- Honorable Mention previous/next links stay inside their category.
- Return links must focus the correct landing-page entry.
- The card-type filter, printings, artwork spotlight, design echo, sources, and navigation must remain functional.
- Site-wide changes require corresponding contract, template, schema, tooling, migration, and validator updates.

- Do not display internal editorial-version, review-status, migration-wave, or approval labels inside published article content. Keep those details in metadata, dossiers, reports, and handoff files.
- Preserve substantive material added during review waves, but present it as normal article content; for example, the Numbered Wave 01 synthesis is titled `Strategic legacy`, not an editorial review.

## Delivery

Every completed wave should include the changed HTML, updated landing page, dossiers, ledgers, validation, review index, report, manifest, checksums, and a mergeable ZIP.
