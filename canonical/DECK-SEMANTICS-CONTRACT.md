# Deck Semantics and Provenance Contract

Arithmetic validity is only the first deck check.

A production deck must also satisfy:

- the article subject appears in the list unless the record documents a historically
  relevant omission or transformation;
- player, event, date, format, placement/significance, and source are supplied when
  claimed;
- every card existed on the stated event date;
- the list obeyed the declared format's rules on that date, or an explicit historical
  exception is documented;
- the mana base can plausibly cast the colored spells;
- a sideboard is never invented to reach fifteen cards;
- the cited source resolves to the represented registration;
- the same deck fingerprint is not recycled across unrelated cards as separate
  evidence.

`editorial-study` is prohibited in production. A curated example must declare its
format, author/reviewer, legality date, and non-tournament status.
