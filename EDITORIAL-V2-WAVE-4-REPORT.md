# Compulsive Research — Editorial V2 Wave 4 Report

**Date:** August 2, 2026  
**Scope:** Four card-specific Editorial V2 reconstructions spanning an Equipment policy landmark, a creature-efficiency benchmark, a combo-control engine, and a multi-format banned one-drop.

## Completed articles

### Umezawa's Jitte

Rebuilt as a Tournament Pillar, Design Landmark, Character/Story Object, and Banned/Restricted Card article. The new version explains charge-counter rules, combat-damage sequencing, the card's connection to Toshiro Umezawa, Standard and Legacy creature wars, and the May 18, 2026 Modern unban. The playbook emphasizes equipment timing, counter conservation, the legend-rule mirror, and the distinction between answering the creature and answering the Equipment.

Historical registrations: Tsuyoshi Fujita at Pro Tour Los Angeles 2005; Olivier Ruel at Pro Tour Honolulu 2006; Ricky Wen's Dead Guy Ale; Marcio Carvalho at Pro Tour 25th Anniversary.

### Tarmogoyf

Rebuilt as a Tournament Pillar and Design Landmark article. It now explains the shared-graveyard card-type calculation, Future Sight's futureshifted design context, Extended and Standard breakthroughs, Modern midrange history, and the gradual displacement of a pure rate creature by threats with immediate card or tempo value. The practical sections cover graveyard engineering, combat math, shrinking effects, and counterplay through exile and type management.

Historical registrations: Remi Fortier at Pro Tour Valencia 2007; Uri Peleg at the 2007 World Championship; Brian Kibler at Pro Tour Austin 2009; David Mines at Mythic Championship IV.

### Splinter Twin

Rebuilt as a Combo/Engine Piece, Tournament Pillar, and Banned/Restricted Card article. The article separates the exact Deceiver Exarch and Pestermite loop from the strategic "Twin tax," traces the archetype from Pro Tour Philadelphia through its mature tempo-control forms, explains the January 2016 Modern ban, and gives concrete guidance on end-step windows, protection, bluffing, removal timing, and post-sideboard transformation.

Historical registrations: Samuele Estratti at Pro Tour Philadelphia 2011; Patrick Dickmann's Tempo Twin; Antonio del Moral Leon at Pro Tour Fate Reforged; Alex Bianchi's Jeskai Twin.

### Deathrite Shaman

Rebuilt as a Tournament Pillar, Design Landmark, and Banned/Restricted Card article. The new version explains why the land-exile activation is targeted and not a mana ability, how the card compressed acceleration, reach, life gain, and graveyard denial, and why Modern banned it in 2014 before Legacy followed in 2018. It also distinguishes its legal but less explosive Pioneer role, where the lack of fetch lands sharply reduces its baseline reliability.

Historical registrations: Yuuya Watanabe at Pro Tour Return to Ravnica; Josh Utter-Leyton at the 2013 World Championship; Javier Dominguez's Grand Prix Paris BUG Delver; Daniel Duterte's Grand Prix Seattle Grixis Delver.
