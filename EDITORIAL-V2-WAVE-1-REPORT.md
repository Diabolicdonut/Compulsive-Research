## Scope and limitations

This is a researched production wave, not a claim that all remaining articles have already been rewritten. Repeating the former bulk-generation approach would recreate the problem Editorial V2 was designed to prevent. The remaining archive should continue through article-specific research dossiers and reviewed batches.

The corpus-wide canonical validator currently reports unrelated legacy identity findings outside these four reviewed pages. Those older findings are not hidden by this wave; the filtered Wave 1 report demonstrates only that the released articles pass their canonical checks.
